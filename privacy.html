Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 7, 2026

Introduction

Folliq ("we," "our," or "us") is owned and operated by Becca Foust. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Folliq mobile application ("the App").

By using Folliq, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Your name, as entered in the profile screen
  • Email address, if provided
  • Photos you submit for hair and scalp analysis, face shape analysis, or hair color matching
  • Client profile information entered by professional users, including names, contact details, hair type notes, color formulas, and undertone profiles
  • Notes and free-text fields you complete within the App

Information Collected Automatically

  • Scan history and analysis results generated by the App
  • App usage data such as features accessed and session frequency
  • Device type and operating system version for technical support purposes

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect Social Security numbers, financial account information, or government identification
  • We do not collect precise location data
  • We do not collect biometric identifiers beyond photos voluntarily submitted for analysis

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide AI-powered hair and scalp analysis, face shape analysis, and hair color matching based on photos you submit
  • To save and display your scan history within the App
  • To enable professional users to manage client profiles and visit records
  • To personalize your experience, such as displaying your name in the App greeting
  • To improve the App and fix technical issues
  • To respond to support requests

How We Store Your Information

On-Device Storage

By default, your scan history, profile information, and client data are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

Cloud Storage

If cloud sync is enabled, your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. Firebase complies with industry-standard security practices including SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certifications.

Photo Storage

Photos submitted for analysis are processed temporarily to generate analysis results. Photos saved to your scan history are stored on your device or in your personal cloud storage. We do not maintain a separate server-side database of your submitted photos.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With AI processing services (OpenAI) solely to generate hair and scalp analysis results, face shape analysis, and hair color matching. Photos are transmitted securely and are subject to OpenAI's privacy policy and data handling practices.
  • With service providers who assist in operating the App, such as cloud infrastructure providers, under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • If required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case users will be notified.

Professional User Responsibilities

If you use Folliq as a licensed cosmetologist, trichologist, or other hair professional to manage client profiles, you are responsible for:

  • Obtaining appropriate consent from your clients before entering their personal information into the App
  • Informing your clients that their hair and scalp photos may be processed by AI systems for analysis purposes
  • Complying with any applicable state or local privacy laws governing client records in your profession

When you submit a client's photo for hair, scalp, face shape, or color analysis within the App, that photo is transmitted to OpenAI for processing in the same manner as photos submitted by personal users. You are responsible for obtaining your client's explicit consent before submitting any photo of them for AI analysis.

Folliq is not a covered entity under HIPAA and is not designed as a HIPAA-compliant platform. Do not enter protected health information as defined by HIPAA into the App.

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Correction

You can access and update your profile information at any time through the Profile screen in the App.

Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Client Data

Professional users can delete individual client profiles at any time from within the client detail screen. Deletion removes all associated data permanently.

Children's Privacy

Folliq is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at folliqcare@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

Third-Party Services

Folliq uses the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • OpenAI — for AI-powered photo analysis including hair and scalp analysis, face shape analysis, and hair color matching. Privacy policy at openai.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — for cloud storage and authentication. Privacy policy at firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat — for subscription and purchase management, including storing subscription status, purchase history, and entitlement data. Privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted data transmission, secure cloud storage, and on-device security. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this document and notify users through the App. Your continued use of Folliq after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
